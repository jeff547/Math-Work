\noindent{}\textbf{Exercise 1:}

Let $X$ denote number of minutes past 10 o'clock that the bus takes to arrive.
Then, $X \sim U(0,30)$, with PDF:
\begin{align*}
    f(x) = \begin{cases}
               \frac{1}{30}, ~ 0 < x < 30 \\
               0, ~ \text{otherwise.}
           \end{cases}
\end{align*}

Since you got here 10 o'clock, if the bus gets here after 10:10, you have waited more than 10 minutes.
That is,
\begin{align*}
    P(\text{wait} > 10 \text{ minutes}) & = P(10 < X < 30)                 \\
                                        & = \int_{10}^{30} \frac{1}{30} dx \\
                                        & = \frac{20}{30}                  \\
                                        & = \frac{2}{3}.
\end{align*}

\noindent{}\textbf{Exercise 3:}

Integrating the entire PDF must be 1. That is,
\begin{align*}
    1 & = \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} f(x) dx \\
      & =\int_0^1 a+bx^2 dx               \\
      & = [ax + \frac{1}{3}bx^3]^1_0      \\
      & = a + \frac{b}{3}.
\end{align*}

Then, using expected value formula, we have
\begin{align*}
    \frac{3}{5} & = \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} x \cdot f(x) dx \\
                & =\int_0^1 ax+bx^3 dx                      \\
                & = [\frac{1}{2}ax^2 + \frac{1}{4}bx^4]^1_0 \\
                & = \frac{a}{2} + \frac{b}{4}.
\end{align*}

Scaling the two equations, we have the following system:
\begin{align*}
    3            & = 3a + b  \\
    \frac{12}{5} & = 2a + b.
\end{align*}

Subtracting the first from the second, we have
\begin{align*}
    a & = 3 - \frac{12}{5} \\
      & = \frac{3}{5}.
\end{align*}

Substituting $a$ into the first equation, we have
\begin{align*}
    \frac{3(3)}{5} + b & = 3               \\
    b                  & = 3 - \frac{9}{5} \\
                       & = \frac{6}{5}.
\end{align*}

Therefore, we have $a = \frac{3}{5}$ and $b = \frac{6}{5}$.
