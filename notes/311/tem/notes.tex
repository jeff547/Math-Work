Chapter 2:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Convergent Sequences:
          \[
              a_n \to a \iff \forall \epsilon > 0, \exists N \in \mathbb{N} \text{ such that } n \geq N \implies |a_n - a| < \epsilon.
          \]

    \item Monotone Convergence Theorem: \( a_n \) is bounded and monotonic \( \implies a_n \) converges.
    \item Bolzano Weierstrass: Every bounded sequence contains a convergent subsequence.
    \item Cauchy Sequences:
          \[
              a_n \text{ is cauchy} \iff \forall \epsilon > 0, \exists N \in \mathbb{N} \text{ such that } n,m \geq N \implies |a_n - a_m| < \epsilon
          \]
    \item Cauchy Criterion: Convergent \( \iff \) Cauchy.

\end{itemize}

\textbf{Practice Midterm 1}
5.
We say that \( a_n \) diverges to \( \infty \) if for every \( M > 0 \)
there exists \( N \in \mathbb{N} \) such that \( n \geq N \implies a_n > M \).
Suppose \( a_n \to \infty  \) and \( b_n \to b > 0 \).
We want to show that
\[
    a_n b_n \to \infty .
\]
Let \( M > 0 \). Since \( b_n \) converges, choose \( N_1 \in \mathbb{N} \) such that
\[
    n \geq N_1 \implies \vert b_n - b \vert  < \frac{b}{2}.
\]
So \( b_n > \frac{b}{2} \) for every \( n \geq N_1 \).
Since \( a_n \) diverges, choose \( N_2 \in \mathbb{N} \) such that
\[
    n \geq N_2 \implies a_n > \frac{2M}{b}.
\]
Set \( N = \max \{N_1, N_2\} \). For every \( n \geq N \) we have
\[
    a_{n} b_n > \frac{2M}{b} \cdot \frac{b}{2} = M.
\]
Therefore \( a_n b_n \to \infty  \).
